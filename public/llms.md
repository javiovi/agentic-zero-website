## Recommended 2026 Editorial Pillars

Agentic Zero should focus on concrete infrastructure questions rather than generic AI news.

### Agents in Production

Core questions:

- What breaks when agents leave demos?
- How should agents use tools, memory, permissions, and policies?
- How do teams evaluate and observe agent behavior?
- What does human-in-the-loop or human-on-the-loop mean in production?
- How do teams make agents reliable enough for financial workflows?

### AI App and Developer Infrastructure

Core questions:

- What does it take to ship AI-native applications?
- How should teams manage latency, cost, reliability, and UX?
- What frontend and backend patterns are emerging for agentic products?
- What developer tools are still missing?

### Open Models, Customization, and Model Behavior

Core questions:

- When do teams choose open models over hosted models?
- What do fine-tuning, post-training, and customization unlock for agents?
- How should teams evaluate model behavior in financial workflows?
- What can open-model communities and closed labs learn from each other?

### The Agentic Finance Stack

Core questions:

- What are the layers needed for agents to become economic actors?
- Which layers are missing or immature?
- Where do AI infrastructure and financial infrastructure meet?

Stack layers:

1. Agent interface and reasoning
2. Tool and MCP access
3. Identity and credentials
4. Discovery and reputation
5. Wallets and custody
6. Authorization and mandates
7. Payment rails
8. Compliance and controls
9. Verification and auditability
10. Institutional integration
11. Dispute resolution and liability

### Agentic Payments and Commerce Rails

Topics:

- x402

- Mastercard Agent Pay
- Stripe agentic commerce
- Coinbase x402
- Circle Agent Stack

### Trust, Identity, Verifiability, and Compliance

## Editorial Filter

Agentic Zero should cover a topic when:

- A serious AI builder would care
- An infrastructure team would see a real product problem
- An open-model researcher would recognize the technical question
- A crypto or financial infrastructure team would understand why on-chain or payment rails matter
- The topic is specific to Agentic Zero, not generic AI coverage

Avoid:

- Generic AI news
- Generic future-of-work posts
- Vague AI x crypto takes
- ChatGPT tips
- Logo-driven posts that do not engage with the technical problems a company cares about
